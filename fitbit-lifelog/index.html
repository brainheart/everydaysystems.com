Fitbit Lifelog

A personal desktop utility maintained by Reinhard Engels for copying his own activity, sleep, and weight records from Google Health into local CSV exports and his Google Sheets lifelog.

The utility requests read-only access to activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements. It does not modify records in Google Health.

This is a private, single-user utility. This website describes the utility; it does not provide a public sign-up or display health data.

Fitbit Lifelog is independently maintained and is not an official Google or Fitbit product.
