Privacy policy

Fitbit Lifelog is maintained by Reinhard Engels exclusively for his own personal recordkeeping.

Access and purpose

With the owner's Google authorization, the utility reads activity and fitness data, sleep data, and health metrics and measurements, including weight. It uses these permissions to retrieve steps, calories burned, sleep times and duration, and weight. A linked health identifier is checked to confirm that the connection works.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored locally with owner-only file permissions. Exported records are retained in local CSV files and selected records are copied into the owner's Google Sheets lifelog. The owner may use authorized software assistants and connected tools to run exports, inspect records, and update that spreadsheet; data used in those operations is processed by those services under their applicable policies and the owner's account settings.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell health data or use it for advertising. This public website contains no health records or OAuth credentials. Local exports and spreadsheet entries remain until the owner deletes them. Deleting the local copies does not delete records held by Google Health or other services.

Control

The owner can revoke access at Google Account connections and delete the local credential files to disconnect the utility. Revocation prevents future authorized retrieval but does not remove existing exports, spreadsheet entries, or records retained by other services.

Questions about this personal utility can be directed to its maintainer using the support email shown on its Google consent screen.
