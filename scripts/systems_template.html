About the dates and timeline

Hover or focus a mark for its title and date; click to open the source. Numbered marks group nearby references: hover, focus, or tap to choose a source. Podcast focus, podcast mention, and web references use separate tracks. Click a References count for the full readable source list. Hover, focus, or tap the timeline heading for its legend. The colored line runs from the first to the latest reference, with gray before and after; it shows the span of documented activity, not continuous use. A single dated reference has a narrow colored marker. Family colors match the podcast table.

References counts the indexed podcast episodes and web pages for each system, counting each source once per date. Click the column heading to sort by total activity. The timeline starts in the year of the earliest indexed source and ends today. Earlier sources can be added as they are verified.
