About the dates and timeline

First mentioned means the earliest dated source indexed here, not a proven date of invention or first publication. Podcasts use the shared episode tags. Web coverage is a curated selection of dated archive posts; undated home pages are linked from system names but are not assigned invented dates.

Hover or focus a mark for its title and date; click to open the source. Numbered marks group nearby references: hover, focus, or tap to choose a source. Podcast focus, podcast mention, and web references use separate tracks. Expand “references” for the full readable list. A line joins the first and latest reference; it does not imply continuous activity. Family colors match the podcast table.

The timeline starts in the year of the earliest indexed source and ends today. Earlier sources can be added as they are verified.
